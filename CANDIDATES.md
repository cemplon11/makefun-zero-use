# Candidate lines — One Line, Zero Use

Judge criteria: funniest first, payoff in the last few words, fresh angle,
tight wording, specific and visual uselessness. Max 200 chars. Must NOT reuse
the rules' examples (toasting bread by staring, remembering every password
except your own) and must avoid the "fly 2 cm" cliché.

## Candidates

1. I can hear what my houseplants are thinking, but only when they're thinking about water.
2. I can teleport, but only to the exact spot I was standing five minutes ago.
3. I can turn any traffic light green — but only after I've already crossed the street.
4. I can speak fluent dolphin, but only to dolphins who are asleep.
5. I can turn invisible, but only when nobody is looking at me anyway.
6. I can predict the future with perfect accuracy — but only about things that happened yesterday.
7. I can open any locked door, but only doors that were already unlocked.
8. I can stop time, but only while I'm asleep.

## Pick

**#3 — "I can turn any traffic light green — but only after I've already crossed the street."**

Why it wins: a real superpower (controlling traffic lights) with a perfectly
useless twist that lands in the last six words; a concrete street-crossing
scene anyone can picture; fresh angle (not the usual precognition/teleport
with-a-catch tropes); every word earns its place. #6 is the runner-up, but
"predict the past" is a more familiar joke shape than #3's timing gag.
